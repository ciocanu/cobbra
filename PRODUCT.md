# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Serious cyclists who want training data depth beyond what Strava or Garmin Connect offer. They've connected Cycplus, Garmin, or Wahoo sensors, track CTL/ATL/TSB, and care about personal records at the 400m and 40km level. Primary context is desktop, at home after a ride, reviewing metrics and progress. Not casual. Not social. Working on something.

## Product Purpose

Cobbra is a hosted cycling analytics platform. Athletes upload .gpx and .fit files, track fitness over time, and get AI-generated analysis and training plans — without social feeds or engagement theatre. Success looks like: complete, private ownership of training data with analytical depth that exceeds free consumer apps.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, focused, no noise. The interface should feel like data you worked for. Voice: precise, direct, confident — a coach who respects your time. The data speaks; the UI gets out of the way. Not warm, not cold. Present.

## Anti-references

- **Strava**: social-first, activity-feed-as-product, engagement-driven, kudos culture
- **Garmin Connect**: cluttered, feature-overflow, legacy desktop feel, every metric competing for attention
- **Generic SaaS dashboards**: metric-hero card templates (big number, small label, gradient accent), identical card grids, hero-metric clichés
- **Gaming / esports dark themes**: neon for neon's sake, aggressive glow everywhere, decoration that has nothing to say
- **Dark mode cliché**: neon blue / purple gradients, glassmorphism as default, the "dark tool" reflex

## Design Principles

1. **Data earned, not decorated.** Every number on screen was produced by actual physical effort. Present it with the same discipline. No embellishment that dilutes signal.
2. **Density without noise.** Serious athletes scan dense information fast. Pack signal tight; remove chrome that doesn't answer a question.
3. **The tool disappears.** Interactions should be predictable enough to fade into background. Flow state is the goal, not feature awareness.
4. **One accent, total commitment.** Cobbra venom green is not decoration — it marks the thing that matters on each screen. Use it sparingly so it retains weight.
5. **Private by design.** User data is not a product. No social feeds, no engagement hooks, no streak mechanics borrowed from consumer apps that treat attention as the output. The service earns trust by doing less.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum: 4.5:1 text contrast, full keyboard navigation, screen reader labels on all interactive elements. Venom green (#39FF14) on near-black achieves exceptional contrast natively — preserve this. Data visualizations (charts, HR zones, goal bars) must not rely on color alone; pair with labels or patterns for color-blind users.
